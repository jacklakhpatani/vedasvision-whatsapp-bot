```
India's largest alleged corporate fraud just happened — and it's a gold company.

SEBI issued an interim order on June 3, 2026 against Rajesh Exports Limited, alleging ₹15.15 lakh crore (approximately $180 billion) in revenue misrepresentation over 5 years.

To put this in perspective: this is larger than India's annual defence budget, and 3,787× bigger than the Harshad Mehta scam.

Here's what the alleged fraud looked like:

1. STRUCTURE
REL (listed) → REL Singapore (no ops) → GGR Switzerland (no ops, never audited) → Valcambi (actual refinery)

Revenue was booked at GGR level — ₹15 lakh crore.
Valcambi's KPMG-audited standalone revenue: ₹3,000 crore over 5 years.

2. THE ACCOUNTING TRICK
Valcambi refines gold — it earns a processing fee.
REL booked the full market value of ALL gold passing through Valcambi as revenue.
This is like a dry cleaner counting the value of every suit they clean as their own sales.

3. FUND DIVERSION
₹339+ crore was moved from company accounts to the promoter's personal account — without board approval — and used for derivative trading.
These trades were then booked as company transactions via a fictitious relationship with "Affluence Shares & Stocks" — which denied any relationship with REL.

4. MISSING ASSETS
₹10,547 crore in "Africa gold mines" that auditors could not verify.
No ownership documents. No location. No valuation. Just a line in the books.

5 compliance takeaways for the UAE gold industry:

→ Revenue recognition: only the refining/processing fee is your revenue
→ Overseas entity audit: every material subsidiary must be independently audited
→ Receivables monitoring: >90 days = escalate, always
→ RPT disclosure: any fund flow to related entities = board approval + disclosure
→ Counterparty verification: confirm every large transaction with the counterparty directly

This case will reshape how regulators globally — including CBUAE, DMCC, and DIFC — scrutinize gold refining companies.

LIC held 10.79% of this company. The question every institutional investor should be asking: what due diligence was done?
```
